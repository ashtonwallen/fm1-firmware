# Tide Clock presets

PRESETS turns through them. The day still shapes the music; a preset recolours it (key, mode, tempo, pad, space,
and AMB-1 Drift 1.1's strings, lead, air and shimmer). Defined in `app/world.c` (`PRESETS[]`).

- **Harbour**: as 1.0 played it.
- **Fjord**: lower, darker, slower. Strings pad, low glass lead, wind.
- **Tropical Atoll**: up a minor third, pentatonic, kalimba, swing.
- **Winter Coast**: darker, slow, strings pad, wind, shimmer.
- **Misty Isles**: up a fourth, choir pad, bowed lead, harp, light rain.
- **Lighthouse Choir**: choir pad, glass lead, long shimmer, little pulse.
- **Sea Glass**: brighter, high bells, kalimba, high glass lead.
- **Gale Warning**: darker, faster, wind, more pulse and bass.
- **Night Ferry**: low, Rhodes, vinyl crackle, swing, bowed lead.
- **Saltmarsh**: harp figures over rain, quieter sea.
- **Long Light**: brighter, strings pad, shimmer, high lead.
- **Chapel Bay**: choir pad, big reverb, slow, shimmer.
- **Kelp Forest**: low and murky, strings pad, echo, low lead.
