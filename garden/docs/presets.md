# GARDEN presets

LFO steps forward, SEL back. Each colours the player's knobs and the garden's own bending; plants keep their pitches
and phrases (steps of the scale). The pad's colour stays the player's. Defined in `app/garden.c` (`PRESETS[]`).

- **Cottage**: as 1.0.
- **Zen**: pentatonic, slow, glass lead, shimmer, a breath of wind.
- **Moonlight**: down a tone, darker, slow, shimmer, high glass lead.
- **Greenhouse**: up a tone, brighter, kalimba, misting rain.
- **Wildflower**: brighter, quicker, harp figures.
- **Herb**: up a fourth, light harp, a breeze.
- **Rain Garden**: steady rain, a few harp notes, more echo.
- **Orchard Wall**: harp, low bowed lead, more bass.
- **Winter Bed**: down a tone, darker, slowest, wind, shimmer, glassy bells.
- **Kitchen Garden**: up a minor third, vinyl crackle, kalimba, echo.
- **Rose Arbour**: brighter, bowed lead, a little shimmer, longer swells.
- **Fern Hollow**: down a minor third, darker, rain, low glass lead.
- **Sundial**: pentatonic, quick, kalimba, drier.
