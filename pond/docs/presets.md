# POND presets

LFO steps forward, SEL back. Each colours the player's Mix and the pond's own shaping; the fish keep their notes (steps
of the scale). Defined in `app/pond.c` (`PRESETS[]`).

- **Mill Pond**: as 1.0. Voices by night, Rhodes by day.
- **Koi Garden**: up a fourth, pentatonic, Rhodes, kalimba.
- **Lily Night**: darker, slower, choir pad, glass lead, shimmer.
- **Mountain Tarn**: lower, darker, slow. Strings pad, wind, low bowed lead.
- **Rain Pond**: rain, a few harp notes, more echo.
- **Frozen Edge**: darker, slowest. Strings pad, high glassy bells, shimmer, wind, no frogs.
- **Lotus Temple**: down a minor third, pentatonic, choir pad, glass lead, shimmer.
- **Willow Bank**: brighter, Rhodes, harp, a breath of wind.
- **Dragonfly Noon**: brighter, faster, kalimba, more pulse and swing.
- **Bog Mist**: low and dark, choir pad, wind, echo, low bowed lead.
- **Moon Pool**: strings pad, shimmer, high glass lead.
- **Spring Thaw**: brighter, light rain, harp, busier bells.
- **Old Well**: low, darker, Rhodes, vinyl crackle, low harp, echo.
